Worst Remaining Failure, Fix & Operational Boundary

Worst Failure & Ranked Fixes Graded Item 5

Dual Framing: False Refusals (Q20, Q23, Q44) worst for utility; 12 Caveat Omissions worst for safety.

Remedy 1: Reranker Ablation (EV Upper Bound: +0.075 Correctness) • Test cross-encoder (~20ms) vs final_k=3 vs prompt relaxation. • Gold chunks are already in top 5; reordering is an untested hypothesis. Remedy 2: Structured Exception Prompting (EV: +0.050 to +0.075) • Formats caveats in bullets; trade-off adds ~600ms latency & +$0.0003 cost.

Concrete Boundary of Safe Use Honest Limitations

  • Permitted: Unsupervised informational FAQ lookup and policy document navigation.
  • Prohibited: Binding claim pre-authorizations or coverage decisions. Omission of minor exclusions (e.g. Senior Care 24-mo pre-existing clause) creates legal liability under IRDAI. Human review mandatory.
  • Prohibited: Unsupervised financial mutation (`issue_refund`). Financial payouts require out-of-band supervisor sign-off via ToolGuard (boundary for future agentic write tools).
  • Prohibited: Unkeyed semantic caching across plans. Collisions at cosine 0.9372.
